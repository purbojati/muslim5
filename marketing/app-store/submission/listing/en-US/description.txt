Stay close to salah, one prayer at a time.

Muslim 5 was built by a fellow Muslim as a gentle companion for the five daily prayers. See local prayer times, find the Qibla, record each salah, and build consistency without guilt. A missed day never erases the effort that came before it.

PRAYER TIMES THAT FOLLOW YOUR DAY
• Location-aware prayer times calculated on your iPhone
• Active and next-prayer countdowns
• Choice of calculation method and Asr method
• Optional reminders for each prayer
• Daylight scenes that change with the prayer phase

QIBLA GUIDANCE
Use the live compass to see the Qibla bearing and simple left-or-right guidance. When you are aligned, the app lets you know. The calculation happens on your iPhone using your location and compass.

SALAH FOCUS
When you choose to enable Salah Focus, Apple’s Screen Time controls place a respectful shield over ordinary apps at prayer time. Complete the salah and record it in Muslim 5 to lift the shield. This feature is optional and never locks the whole iPhone.

A GENTLE SALAH JOURNEY
• Record completed, late, or prayed-after-time status
• Note congregation or individual prayer
• See streaks, complete days, and 30-day consistency
• Look back over a 20-week prayer grid
• Receive reflections that encourage returning, not giving up
• Pause tracking with Period Mode when needed

SALAH CIRCLE
Pray together, even when apart. Link privately with family or close friends using a code, then see their initials beneath a prayer after they check in. There are no messages, rankings, public profiles, or location sharing.

PRIVACY FIRST
Your detailed prayer history, prayer times, location, streaks, and preferences are not sent to the Salah Circle service. Prayer history and Period Mode may sync through your private iCloud account. Salah Circle only uses the minimum data required to link people and show completed-prayer check-ins. You can unlink people or delete your sharing profile from the app.

No ads. No judgment. Just a calm place to keep returning to salah, inshaAllah.
